# Zoella Balloons — Luxury Event Architecture & Decor

> Premier custom balloon styling, organic garlands, and architectural event backdrops in Houston, Texas.

## 🌟 Highlights
- **Design System**: "Thick & Alive" tactile elevation, custom champagne/cabernet harmonic palette, and responsive mobile architecture.
- **GSAP Motion Suite**: Staggered hero reveal, parallax imagery, KPI counter ticker, magnetic interactive CTAs, and architectural lines.
- **2026 AI-Native SEO & Local Geo**: Machine-readable `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`, explicit AI crawler directives in `robots.txt`, XML `sitemap.xml`, Schema.org (`LocalBusiness` & `ProfessionalService`), and semantic `<noscript>` fallback.
- **Inquiry Generator**: In-browser booking enquiry compiler with 1-click clipboard copy for direct Instagram DM handoff to [@zoellaballoons](https://www.instagram.com/zoellaballoons/).
- **Houston Metropolitan Coverage**: Integrated Google Maps venue verification for River Oaks, Memorial, The Heights, Montrose, Galleria, Katy, Sugar Land, and The Woodlands.
